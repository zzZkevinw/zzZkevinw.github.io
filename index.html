About me

I received my Master of Computer Science from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and my B.S. in Computer Science from Rutgers University.

My research interests lie in robotics and embodied AI, with a focus on vision-language-action (VLA) models. My current work studies distribution shifts and failure prediction to improve the reliability of VLA models during deployment.
